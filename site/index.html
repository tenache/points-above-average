How it works

PAA per attempt = the player's points per attempt − the league's points per attempt. Positive is better than average. If the league scores 1 point per attempt, a made 2 is +1, and a made 2 followed by a miss is 0.

An attempt is a field-goal attempt or one trip to the free-throw line: two or three free throws in a trip count as one attempt, so 2 of 2 from the line is worth the same as a made 2. An and-one is one attempt (the free throw adds a point to the shot). Technical and flagrant free throws are left out, points included. Turnovers don't count.

The league average is every player's points over every player's attempts this season, counted the same way. Last night's numbers use the average through that night, so they don't change later.

Total PAA in the leaderboard is the running total: points − league average × attempts. It rewards volume: a great shooter who shoots a lot piles it up.

Who's on the boards: players with at least 5 points per team game, his season points over the games his team has played. A good bench player qualifies, and a player who stops playing drops off: 45 points in game 1 stays on for 9 team games.

Versus teammates compares a player with the rest of his team in the games he played, so a player on a bad shooting team doesn't get punished for his teammates, and vice versa. Traded players get one row per team.

Data from Basketball-Reference play-by-play, fetched once a day.
